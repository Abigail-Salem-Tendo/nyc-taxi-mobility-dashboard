Insight 1: Rush Hour Volume vs Efficiency

Observation: Trip volume peaks at 8am and 6pm.

Pattern: Avg speed drops and fare per mile is lower during peak hours.

Recommendation: High volume ≠ high efficiency. Spreading demand to off-peak hours improves revenue per mile for drivers.

Insight 2: Distance & Short Trip Inefficiency

Observation: A large share of trips are short-distance.

Risk: Short trips increase congestion while lowering system throughput.

Policy Implication: Incentivizing pooled or micro-mobility alternatives may improve efficiency.

Insight 3: Borough-Level Performance

Observation: Trip density varies significantly by borough.

Pattern: High trip density correlates with lower average speeds.

Recommendation: Borough-specific congestion strategies are needed.
